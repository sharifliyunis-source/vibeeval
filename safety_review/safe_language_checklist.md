# Safe Language Checklist

## Middle Corridor Brief — Editorial and Safety Standards

---

## Purpose

This checklist ensures every Middle Corridor Brief newsletter maintains institutional-grade neutrality. It is used by Agent 6 (Editorial and Safety Reviewer) and by the automated `safety_scan.py` script.

A newsletter requires a minimum safety score of **7/10** before distribution.

## Safety Scoring Criteria

Agent 6 and `safety_scan.py` score each newsletter on a 1–10 scale:

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Fully safe; institutional-grade language throughout |
| 7–8 | Safe with minor adjustments; acceptable for publication |
| 5–6 | Moderate issues; requires revision before publication |
| 3–4 | Significant risky language; major revision required |
| 1–2 | Fundamental framing problems; full rewrite required |

**Minimum publication threshold: 7/10**

---

## Sensitive Topics Requiring Extra Caution

The following topics require additional editorial review regardless of language used:

1. **Kazakhstan–Russia relations** — Focus on infrastructure and trade only
2. **China's BRI investments in corridor countries** — Neutral, development-finance framing only
3. **US/EU sanctions on Russia and Iran** — Do not reference in relation to corridor routing
4. **Azerbaijan–Armenia relations** — Focus only on infrastructure; no territorial framing
5. **Caspian legal status disputes** — Infrastructure focus only; no sovereign claim language
6. **Xinjiang transit routes** — Not within this project's scope; avoid entirely

## Final Publication Checklist (Agent 6)

Before approving any issue for distribution:

- [ ] No prohibited framing identified in the checklist above
- [ ] All major claims are sourced and confirmed
- [ ] Executive tone maintained throughout
- [ ] Green energy claims clearly distinguish confirmed from speculative
- [ ] No military or security framing present
- [ ] No regime or political criticism
- [ ] Development finance section uses neutral institutional language
- [ ] Disclaimer present and correctly worded
- [ ] Safety score 7/10 or above
- [ ] Final version reviewed by Agent 7 after Agent 6 revisions
